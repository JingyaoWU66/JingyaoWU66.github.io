Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence

Understanding people through AI.

I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the Affective Computing group at the MIT Media Lab and an MIT–Novo Nordisk AI Postdoctoral Fellow, working with Prof. Rosalind W. Picard.

My research develops human-centered AI that accounts for the ambiguity, complexity, and dynamics of human behavior. I work across affective computing, speech processing, multimodal learning, and behavioral time-series modeling.

Research

Research interests

01

Ambiguity

Building AI that recognizes uncertainty, disagreement, and subjectivity as meaningful parts of human experience.

02

Complexity

Integrating speech, language, vision, and physiological signals to understand people across multiple modalities and contexts.

03

Dynamics

Modeling how behavior, emotion, and well-being unfold over time for more adaptive and personalized systems.

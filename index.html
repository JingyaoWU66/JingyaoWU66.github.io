About

I am Jingyao, and my friends and professors also call me Jennifer. I am currently a Postdoctoral Associate at the MIT Media Lab, awarded the MIT–Novo Nordisk AI Postdoctoral Fellowship (Jan 2025 – Jan 2027), hosted by Prof. Rosalind Picard.

I was born in a small city in northern China and moved to Sydney, Australia at the age of 17 to pursue my studies abroad. Since then, my journey has taken me across continents in pursuit of research, learning, and new experiences.

I received my BE (Hons) degree with First Class Honours in Telecommunication Engineering from the University of New South Wales (UNSW) in 2020. During my undergraduate studies, I attended my first international conference, ICASSP 2019 in Brighton, UK, which inspired my journey toward a PhD.

I completed my PhD in Signal Processing at UNSW in 2024 under the supervision of Associate Professor Vidhyasaharan Sethu, Professor Eliathamby Ambikairajah, and Dr Ting Dang.

My research focuses on ambiguity-aware and human-centered AI, affective computing, multimodal signal processing, and temporal modeling of human behavior. My work has been published in venues including ICASSP, INTERSPEECH, ACII, and IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing. My publications include a Best Paper Award at ACII 2023 and a paper recognized among the Top 3% of ICASSP 2023 submissions.

I truly cherish the experiences I’ve had throughout this journey, and I remain open to exploring and embracing new opportunities in the future. I am actively seeking faculty positions — please feel free to reach out.

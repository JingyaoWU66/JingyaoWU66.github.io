01 / Research

AI for the
messiness of life.

My work sits at the intersection of signal processing, machine learning, and affective computing. I build models that treat ambiguity as meaningful information—not noise to be removed.

01

Ambiguity-aware AI

Understanding how uncertainty, disagreement, and subjectivity shape AI decisions in affective computing and mental health.

02

Temporal modeling

Tracking emotional states and well-being over time to support personalized monitoring and early forecasting.

03

Speech & multisensory intelligence

Combining speech, language, vision, and physiological signals for robust, context-aware models of human behavior.

About

I am Jingyao, and my friends and professors also call me Jennifer. I am currently working as a Postdoctoral Associate at the MIT Media Lab, awarded the MIT–Novo Nordisk AI Postdoctoral Fellowship (Jan 2025 – Jan 2027), hosted by Prof. Rosalind Picard.

I was born on February 14th, 1998, in a small city in northern China. At the age of 17, I moved to Sydney, Australia to pursue my studies abroad, beginning a journey across continents toward becoming an international scholar and researcher.

I received my BE (Hons) degree with First Class Honours in Telecommunication Engineering from the School of Electrical Engineering and Telecommunications at the University of New South Wales (UNSW) in 2020. During my undergraduate studies, I attended my first international conference, ICASSP 2019 in Brighton, UK, which inspired my journey toward pursuing a PhD.

I completed my PhD in Signal Processing at UNSW in 2024 under the supervision of Associate Professor Vidhyasaharan Sethu, Professor Eliathamby Ambikairajah, and Dr Ting Dang.

My research focuses on ambiguity-aware and human-centered AI, affective computing, multimodal signal processing, temporal modeling of human behavior, and multisensory intelligence.

My work has been published in ICASSP, INTERSPEECH, ACII, and IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing. My publications include a Best Paper Award at ACII 2023 and a paper recognized among the Top 3% of ICASSP 2023 submissions.

I truly cherish the experiences I’ve had throughout this journey, and I remain open to exploring and embracing new opportunities in the future. I am actively seeking faculty positions — please feel free to reach out.
